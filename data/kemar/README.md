# MIT KEMAR HRTF measurements (compact set, elevation 0°)

37 stereo impulse responses (`elev0/H0e{azimuth:03d}a.wav`, 0–180° in 5° steps,
44.1kHz, 128 taps) from the MIT Media Lab KEMAR dummy-head measurements.
Left-side azimuths are obtained by swapping the ear channels (see
`shadowweave/audio/hrtf.py`).

Source: https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR/compact.zip

Citation: Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR
Dummy-Head Microphone," MIT Media Lab Perceptual Computing Technical Report
#280, May 1994. The data is free for use provided the authors are cited.
